Our Trips

Lazy Canyon Float

A relaxed, mostly flat paddle through the shaded stretch of Kolob Canyon. Perfect for first-timers, young kids, and anyone who wants scenery over adrenaline. Keep an eye out for herons and the occasional deer along the banks.

Class: I–II  ·  Length: 2 hours  ·  Best for: Families & first-timers

Rapid Runner Half-Day

Our most popular trip. A steady mix of splashy class III rapids and calmer pools to catch your breath, led by a guide who'll teach you to read the water as you go. Great for groups who want a real workout without an all-day commitment.

Class: III  ·  Length: 4 hours  ·  Best for: First-time thrill seekers

Big Water Full-Day

An all-day run through the roughest water we guide, including back-to-back class IV rapids and a riverside lunch stop. Built for experienced paddlers who want to earn every calm stretch in between.

Class: IV  ·  Length: 8 hours  ·  Best for: Experienced rafters
